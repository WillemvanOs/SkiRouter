What we keep, and why

  • Email address and password (the password only as a secure hash): to sign you in. Basis: the contract with you (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Date of birth: to check you are 16 or older, the age from which you may use an online service without a parent's consent (GDPR art. 8, Dutch UAVG art. 5). Basis: legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Country: for the right prices and tax (VAT) on the season pass. Basis: legal obligation and the contract.
  • Preferences (piste colours, pace, drag lifts, ski bus, favourite ski areas, lifts and pistes you avoid) and saved days: so they come with you on every phone. Basis: the contract.
  • Payments for the season pass: date, amount, status and the payment's reference at Mollie. We never see your bank or card details. Basis: the contract, and the legal duty to keep financial records (7 years).

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it and do not build profiles. Your location, when you use "near me" or start from where you are, stays on your phone.

How long

As long as you have an account. When you delete it (Account → Delete my account), your profile, preferences and saved days are deleted at once. Payment records are kept for 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires.
